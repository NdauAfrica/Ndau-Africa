// Infrastructure & Impact

Making Invisible Capability Auditable

Ndau Africa builds AYLI — a multimodal AI protocol that verifies the real, demonstrated skills of Africa's informal workers and informal business owners, mapping evidence from job-site video and photos directly to the African Continental Qualifications Framework (ACQF).

600 million economically active Africans remain invisible to formal employment, bank credit, and institutional contracts — not because they lack technical expertise, but because no trusted system exists to audit what they know.

AYLI bridges this gap by converting real physical work into auditable, institution-ready data credentials: Individual Skill Passports for skilled workers, and Enterprise Passports for informal business owners.

// Verification Architecture

Human-Verified, AI-Accelerated

AYLI relies on a disciplined three-layer verification approach to make skill outputs trustworthy for institutional lenders and general contractors.

01

Multimodal Video Extraction

Gemini AI ingests raw job-site footage to detect tools, techniques, safety compliance, and physical execution speed in real work environments.

02

Adversarial Output Auditing

Automated checks flag anomalies, unnatural edits, or staged setups to protect credential integrity against spoofing or hallucinated skill levels.

03

Expert & Peer Validation

Human-in-the-loop trade experts and verified peer assessments anchor machine findings to ground-truth craftsmanship standards.

🛡️ Data Governance Notice: Built with POPIA-aligned data governance, end-to-end media encryption, and explicit worker consent protocols at the core of every capture.

// Leadership & Origins

The Founder

Arnold Muza

Founder & CEO — Ndau Africa

Originally from Chipinge, Zimbabwe, Arnold learned early that formal credentials rarely reflect true technical capability. Self-taught through relentless discipline following Form 4, he moved to Johannesburg and built a hands-on home renovation business operating daily on active job sites.

Working directly alongside master bricklayers, carpenters, and electricians who possessed world-class skill but zero formal documentation, he recognized the structural gap: a complete lack of institutional legibility. Driven to solve this, Arnold engineered the protocol's first working AI verification pipeline himself.
