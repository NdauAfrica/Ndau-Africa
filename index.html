Market Imperfection

The Economic Legibility Gap

Over 80% of urban infrastructure, trade, and technical work across Africa is executed by informally trained, master-level tradespeople. Because their skills exist outside traditional educational registries, capital deployers treat them as high-risk.

01 / CAPITAL

Lending Asymmetry

Commercial banks want to fund micro-enterprises, but verifying an informal contractor’s operational capacity costs more than the loan itself.

02 / PROCUREMENT

Prime Risk Exposure

Large infrastructure developers are required to use local subcontractors but lack reliable tools to audit technical capability beforehand.

03 / INSURANCE

Unquantified Liability

Insurers cannot price micro-liability policies for job sites without verified safety logs and asset audit histories.

Protocol Framework

The African Labour Intelligence Protocol (AYLI)

AYLI operates as a continuous discovery, verification, and risk-scoring engine designed to bridge informal execution with formal economic capital.

1. Discover

Ingests unscripted job-site video and visual telemetry directly from working tradespeople.

2. Verify

Applies multimodal models to audit physical technique, safety postures, and tool configurations.

3. Activate

Maps evidence directly to Continental Qualification Frameworks (ACQF) and enterprise profiles.

4. Predict

Generates commercial reliability scores to unlock bank credit, insurance, and prime supply chains.

Multimodal Stack

4-Layer Verification Framework

To produce institutional-grade intelligence from raw media, AYLI processes data across four distinct audit layers:

LAYER 1
Raw Evidence Telemetry Metadata-tagged visual records, site coordinates, equipment inventory, and client timestamps.
LAYER 2
AI-Assisted Spatial & Technical Intelligence (Gemini Core) Multimodal reasoning evaluates tool selection, safety protocol compliance, joint precision, and workflow velocity.
LAYER 3
Adversarial & Human Audit Pass Adversarial prompt checks prevent hallucination while accredited regional trade experts validate edge cases.
LAYER 4
Economic Reliability Index (API Output) Structured JSON outputs containing financial reliability scores, equipment valuation, and ACQF skill equivalencies.
