Leadership & Origin

Founder Story & Operator Insight

Arnold Muza

Founder & CEO — Ndau Africa

Raised by his grandparents in Chipinge, Zimbabwe, Arnold left formal school after Form 4 and arrived in Johannesburg without paper credentials. He self-taught hotel management and ran an establishment by age 22 before founding a residential renovation enterprise in South Africa.

Arnold did not discover the informal labor market from academic research—he discovered it while building businesses directly alongside master welders, electricians, and builders. In his native roots, the word Ndau signifies “our place” or “our land.” He recognized that the people literally building Africa's infrastructure have been left without economic standing simply because traditional systems have no tools to measure their true capability.

Combining his field-level trade operations with self-taught AI pipeline development, Arnold built AYLI's first multimodal verification model to establish the trust infrastructure Africa's informal workforce requires to access capital, credit, and enterprise growth.
